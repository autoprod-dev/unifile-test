Fonts used by Unifile for PDF output

- Carlito (metric-compatible with Calibri) and Caladea (metric-compatible with Cambria): SIL Open Font Licence 1.1. Embedded, compressed, in index.html.
- DejaVu Sans (subset for Greek, Cyrillic, Hebrew, Arabic, symbols): Bitstream Vera / DejaVu licence. Embedded, compressed, in index.html.
- Noto Sans CJK (split into fonts/cjk-00..09.ttf) and Noto Emoji (fonts/emoji.ttf): SIL Open Font Licence 1.1. Loaded only when a PDF needs Chinese, Japanese or Korean text, or emoji.
- @pdf-lib/fontkit: MIT.
Fonts were subset or re-saved (glyph table padding) for the browser. No other changes.
